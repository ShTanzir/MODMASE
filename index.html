FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is MODMASE?

MODMASE is a Telegram channel and website for Android MODs and tweaks, useful apps and tools, games and game mods, the latest updates and releases, and modding resources and guides.

How do I join MODMASE?

Open t.me/MODMASE or tap the Join on Telegram button on this page, then join the channel in the Telegram app to get every new post.

Is there a MODMASE Android app?

Yes. The MODMASE app brings the channel banner, one-tap links to Telegram and this website, channel info and a small toolbox with a device monitor and quick shortcuts. Announcements and downloads are shared on the Telegram channel.

Is MODMASE free?

Joining the MODMASE Telegram channel and browsing this website are free.

How can I stay safe when installing mods?

Keep a backup of the original app, download files only from sources you trust, check the permissions an app asks for, keep Google Play Protect turned on, and read the notes in each channel post before installing.

No. MODMASE is an independent project. Android is a trademark of Google LLC and Telegram is a trademark of Telegram FZ-LLC.
