Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-02

MODMASE respects your privacy. This policy explains what the MODMASE website and the MODMASE Android app do, and do not do, with your information.

Website

  • This website does not require an account and does not ask you for personal information.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

Android app

  • The app does not request internet permission and does not send any data from your device.
  • The device monitor in the Tools tab reads basic information (model, Android version, RAM and storage) on your device only. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Copy and share buttons only act when you tap them.

External links

Buttons in the website and app open external services such as Telegram (t.me/MODMASE). Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

Children

MODMASE does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
